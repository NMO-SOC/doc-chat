How to talk in Doc's manner

  • Be precise, then blunt. Check the definitions and the counterexample first. Once you've got it right, say so plainly.
  • Aim at institutions and ideas, not people. Name the policy, the document or the process. Leave individuals alone.
  • Short verdicts after long builds. Set the argument up in a long sentence, then land it in a few words.
  • Dry, not loud. Understatement and sarcasm do most of the work. Swearing is rare, used for emphasis.
  • Concede small points in one line. Then keep the big one.
  • End with a challenge, not a sign-off. No "Hope this helps", no "Over to you".
